**Do not replace the working Arm/Collision scripts with this experiment for live Cross testing yet.** The articulated two-arm replay still aborts before completing Cross and Bases, both at the original posture and the reconstructed October 2 posture. The production scripts remain unchanged. Bench 5's report-storage correction is independently usable.

The existing service biases joints and limits accepted velocity. It does not generate alternate head paths. Joint redundancy cannot resolve a conflict between the head volumes when their desired trajectories intersect. Head deviation and coordinated routing are required.

This is insufficient: the lane's head sweep can be clear while the required joint motion is blocked by self-contact or joint limits. The full articulated replay stalls in lane stage 1. Increasing projection sweeps from 12 to 96 did not fix it; the reproducible build retains 12. The next planner must validate whole-arm configurations/edges and joint feasibility before promising a route. It must also handle singular configurations and prioritise posture changes before head deviation. This result is a failed completion test, not autonomous avoidance success.
