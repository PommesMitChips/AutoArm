# Panel assembler

Scope: a local, offline utility for the existing SVG panel pack. Mode: Operate.
Keep original drawings and copy. No replacement identity or concept round is
needed for this precisely specified utility.

## Direction contract

THESIS: arrange the actual guide panels and see the export immediately.
OWN-WORLD: existing Oxanium/Inter lettering, steel surfaces, amber actions;
flat lists and labelled controls.
STORY: drag thumbnail tiles into assemblies, arrange stacks or wider Posters,
stage colour selections in orange/yellow and confirm them, save and export.
FIRST VIEWPORT: filename/thumbnail grid at left, independently scrolling
assembly pane in the middle, zoomable/pannable artwork at right. Export remains
in the toolbar; selection controls and notifications overlay the bottom right.
FORM: direct utility extension of the approved documentation; no seed because
the request specifies a narrow tool inside the existing visual world.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
