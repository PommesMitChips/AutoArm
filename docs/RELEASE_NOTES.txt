AutoArm 3.1 — Predictable tool return, linear docking and expected transitions

ToolSwap records the actual parked position/orientation relative to a stand while
its merge pairs are connected. The record survives restart and is validated by
marker/merge identities and rigid layout. Returning to that rack preserves the
original rest pose rather than choosing a visually indistinguishable quarter turn.
Start with all tools parked; no manual orientation input or teaching is required.

Travel uses HeadSpeed and HeadTurnSpeed from the arm PB. ToolSwap MoveSpeed and
TurnSpeed apply only to final docking/insertion and withdrawal. Optional per-point
policies select inherited arm speeds. Manual correction caps no longer impose a
hidden ceiling on autonomous travel; physical actuator/acceleration bounds remain.
Linear stages align first, hold orientation and track a reference on the captured
Cartesian segment. Completion checks the endpoint, including very slow movement.

Before native merge/mount changes, the coordinator declares a stopped transition.
The arm pauses stale topology without canceling the swap. Once separation/attachment
is observed, it rewalks and settles the arm, then activates control on a fresh timed
update. Source support merges stay disabled during travel to prevent early snap.
Unexpected upstream changes still stop. Collision avoidance remains outside scope.
