AutoArm 4.0.6 — Approach arrival and merge capture

The reported stall was still Tools ApproachDock: roughly 10mm from the
clearance waypoint, against a 5mm/0.2degree configuration, with 0.019m/s requested.
That waypoint incorrectly required final docking precision and near-zero motion.

ToolSwap now marks its paths with optional Docking policy. Intermediate arrival
has separate distance/angle and low-speed admission, bounded for short approach
segments. It carries motor output into insertion rather than imposing another
zero-output stop. Far insertion allows small alignment error, progressively
returning to the requested precision near the coupler. Large error pauses forward
advance; final rotor identity/alignment and stable attachment proofs remain.

Final docking measured-speed thresholds scale with the existing pose tolerance
instead of always requiring 0.005m/s and 0.2degrees/s. Final pose precision still
uses the configured values. Generic paths retain their previous strict policy.

Merge parking ends at a small outward capture gap: up to 3cm, bounded to a quarter
of ApproachDistance. After confirmed stopped output, head merges are enabled
and native capture completes docking. The exact saved mating pose is unchanged;
fixtures now model native cell snap and physical ownership across pointer gaps.

Update BOTH scripts and run On. Existing Custom Data, including the supplied
5mm/0.2degree final settings, can be kept. No new setup parameters are required.
Arm Format 7, ToolSwap Format 2 and protocol 5 remain unchanged. Manual control
and PID gains are unchanged.

Validation: installed SE interfaces/C#6, specialization, exact packing method
IL, and 195,302 simulated assertions. Cases cover the reported clearance stall,
strict generic paths, excessive entry speed, short-approach refusal, graded
far/near alignment, bounded jitter, dangerous final velocity/pose rejection,
capture gap and complete two-PB/multi-arm swaps. Live game collision/contact
and magnetic behavior still need confirmation on the user's arm.

Arm: 99,843 characters, 157 free. ToolSwap: 65,240, 34,760 free.
